Important notice

Terms & Conditions

  • Safe Harem is designed for girls between the ages of 13 and 25.
  • All users must respect privacy, kindness, and consent in conversations.
  • Messages are intended for emotional support, guidance, and learning, not for dangerous or abusive use.
  • Users should never share confidential personal details that they are not comfortable sharing.
  • In urgent situations, users should contact a trusted adult, local emergency support, or a certified professional.
  • We encourage users to seek advice from trusted people, such as NGOs, faith leaders, and community mentors.
  • Safe Harem promotes respectful, safe, and inclusive support for all young people.
